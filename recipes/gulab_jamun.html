Gulab Jamun

Description

Gulab jamun is a classic Indian dessert consisting of fried milk solids-based dough balls soaked in a sweet, sticky sugar syrup flavored with cardamom and rose water. Perfect for festivals and special occasions!

Ingredients

  • khoya or milk powder
  • all-purpose flour
  • ghee or oil for deep frying
  • sugar syrup flavored with cardamom and rose water

Steps

  1. Make the sugar syrup
  2. Knead the milk solids dough
  3. Shape into smooth small balls
  4. Heat the ghee or oil
  5. Deep fry on low heat
  6. Drain the fried balls
  7. Soak in warm sugar syrup
  8. Rest until soft and juicy
  9. Serve warm or chilled
