Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe is made with an easy meat sauce as the base. Layer the sauce with noodles and cheese, then bake until bubbly! This is great for feeding a big family and freezes well, too.

Ingredients

  • noodles
  • a rich meat and tomato sauce
  • a creamy cheese filling
  • plenty of melted topping

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles
  2. Brown the meat
  3. Simmer The Sauce
  4. Mix the cheese filling
  5. Layer the lasagna
  6. Top with cheese
  7. Bake covered
  8. Bake uncovered
  9. Rest the lasagna
