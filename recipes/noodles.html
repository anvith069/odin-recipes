Stir-Fry Noodles

Description

A quick and flavorful stir-fry noodle dish packed with crisp vegetables and tossed in a savory savory soy-based sauce. It is a perfect weeknight dinner that comes together in less than 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • egg noodles or ramen noodles
  • mixed vegetables like cabbage and carrots
  • savory stir-fry sauce
  • garlic and green onions for garnish

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles
  2. Drain and rinse with cold water
  3. Whisk the stir-fry sauce ingredients
  4. Chop the vegetables and aromatics
  5. Heat oil in a large wok
  6. Stir-fry the garlic and vegetables
  7. Add the noodles to the wok
  8. Pour in the sauce and toss
  9. Garnish and serve hot
